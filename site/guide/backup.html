Loops, macros, remaps and settings are kept in %AppData%\PuppyMacro, so every new version finds them. In Settings > Backup:

  • Export… saves everything to one .puppymacro file, to move it to another PC or keep a copy.
  • Import… replaces your loops, macros, remaps and settings with the ones in a file, after you confirm with Import and restart. Your current data is saved first as a backup in the data folder, then PuppyMacro restarts.
  • Open data folder opens %AppData%\PuppyMacro.
