PuppyMacro works with the AI apps on your PC that support MCP (Model Context Protocol), like Claude Desktop, Visual Studio Code with GitHub Copilot and Codex. Ask in your own words, for example "in Notepad, make Ctrl+X type A" or "make a macro that opens my inventory and clicks the first slot": the AI prepares the loop, macro or remap and PuppyMacro opens it in its editor, where you check it and save it. The AI and its account are your AI app's: PuppyMacro has no AI of its own.

Set it up

  1. Select AI in the side rail, above Settings, and turn on Allow AI access. While it is off, AI apps can connect but every request is refused, and the AI tells you to turn it on.
  2. Under Apps, select Connect on your app:
    • Claude Desktop: PuppyMacro is added to Claude Desktop's settings (claude_desktop_config.json). Restart Claude Desktop: quit it from its icon in the system tray, then open it again. Use the Claude Desktop app: claude.ai in the browser can't reach PuppyMacro.
    • Visual Studio Code: VS Code opens on the page that installs PuppyMacro's MCP server: select Install there. Then ask in Copilot Chat in Agent mode, with the model you choose there.
    • Codex: PuppyMacro is added to Codex's settings (%UserProfile%\.codex\config.toml). Restart Codex.
    • Other apps: Copy configuration copies the settings to paste in the MCP settings of any app that supports local MCP servers (the usual mcpServers format), then restart that app.
  3. Once the app has started PuppyMacro's server, its tile says Connected since and the time. Before that it says, for example, Added. Restart Claude Desktop to connect. If PuppyMacro is not running, the AI app starts it in the system tray.

Remove takes PuppyMacro out of that app's settings. If VS Code's mcp.json has comments, PuppyMacro does not rewrite it: remove PuppyMacro in VS Code with MCP: Open User Configuration. The buttons are off while Allow AI access is off.

What you can ask

  • Questions: which loops, macros and remaps you have, what one does, whether a key or combination is free, how something works in PuppyMacro (the AI reads this guide, of the version you have).
  • New or changed loops, macros and remaps: the loop, macro or remap editor opens filled in, with Proposed by and the app's name at the top. Change what you want, then select Save, or Cancel. Nothing is saved until you select Save, and the editor checks it as when you make it yourself. The AI is told what was saved, also the changes you made.
  • Delete, enable or disable a loop, macro or remap, and change settings (theme, close to system tray, the global hotkeys, the overlay panel, its opacity, clicks in the panel, sound volume): a PuppyMacro window, Asked by the app, lists the change; it happens only if you confirm.
  • Points, apps and keys: instead of guessing, the AI can ask you to click a point on the screen (for a click or a move; Esc cancels), to click a window of an app (for Specific app), or to press a key or combination.
  • Stop all: the AI can stop every running loop and macro.

How requests show up

  • PuppyMacro's window for a request opens on top of the other apps, also while PuppyMacro is in the system tray; the main window stays hidden. If Windows only flashes it in the taskbar, select it there.
  • One request that needs you at a time. A window closes by itself, with nothing changed, when nobody uses PuppyMacro for 5 minutes (any key, click or move in its windows counts).
  • While you have an editor or a dialog of PuppyMacro open, the AI is told to wait until you close it. Loops and macros can't be changed while something runs: stop them first (or the AI can use Stop all).

What AI apps cannot do

  • Start or test loops and macros: their keys and clicks would go to the window in front, usually the AI app's chat. Start them yourself, with the Play button on their card, their hotkey or overlay mode.
  • Change Start with Windows, Allow AI access, backups and updates.
  • Reach PuppyMacro from a website: claude.ai, ChatGPT and other AI apps in the browser or on the phone can't connect to an app on your PC. Use an app installed on the PC.

Activity

Recent activity on the AI page shows the last requests: when, which app, what it asked and how it ended (Saved, Saved with changes, Cancelled, Refused…). View all opens the AI activity window, with Period, App, Type, Result and Search; select a request to see what was proposed or saved. The activity is kept for 30 days on this PC; Clear history deletes it.

Privacy

PuppyMacro sends nothing to AI services. What the AI app reads through PuppyMacro (your loops, macros, remaps and settings, the names of the open apps, the points and keys you give) goes to that app's provider, under its own privacy policy. Turn off Allow AI access to stop every request.
