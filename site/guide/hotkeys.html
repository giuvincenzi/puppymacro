• Global hotkeys, in Settings > Hotkeys: Stop all (Alt+Shift+S on a new install) stops every running loop and macro, Overlay mode (Alt+Shift+W on a new install) switches between the window and the overlay, Record (F8) starts a recording after you select Record macro, and stops it. To change one, click its keys and press the new hotkey (Esc cancels); the arrow next to the keys chooses the side of each Ctrl, Alt, Shift and Win. They cannot be removed. After an update, your hotkeys stay as they were.
  • At the bottom of the main window, Enable overlay mode and Stop all show their hotkeys. Stop all is red while something runs and off otherwise.
  • Everywhere PuppyMacro asks for a key or hotkey, the field works the same way: Set hotkey (or Set key) when there is none; while it waits, a turning ring and “Press … (Esc cancels)”; once set, the keys in the accent color. Click the keys to change them. The arrow next to the keys always has Change; where the key can be removed (the hotkey of a loop or macro, a loop's key row) also Clear; and for each Ctrl, Alt, Shift and Win in it, its side (see below).
  • A hotkey is a key or mouse button, optionally with Ctrl, Alt, Shift or Win. It matches exactly: F6 does not trigger on Ctrl+F6.
  • Ctrl, Alt, Shift and Win have a left and a right key. A hotkey you press counts only with the side you used: choose Left Ctrl+F6 and the right Ctrl with F6 does nothing, so the right one is free for another hotkey. To have either side count, open the arrow next to the keys and choose Left or right Ctrl (or Left Ctrl, Right Ctrl to change the side); the keys then show Ctrl. Hotkeys and remaps saved by earlier versions work with either side, as before. Two hotkeys clash unless a modifier is on opposite sides: Left Ctrl+F6 and Right Ctrl+F6 can both exist, Ctrl+F6 clashes with both.
  • With Ctrl, Alt, Shift or Win held, left and right click, the scroll wheel (up, down, left, right) and Esc can be hotkeys too, for example Ctrl+left click or Alt+wheel down. Alone they cannot: they would block the mouse, scrolling or Esc everywhere. While you choose a hotkey, a click alone still clicks, the wheel alone still scrolls and Esc alone cancels.
  • A scroll wheel hotkey counts every notch of the wheel as one press. On a touchpad or a wheel that scrolls smoothly, the movement of one notch counts as one press. The wheel cannot be held down, so it works only with Toggle, not with Hold.
  • Hotkeys and remap source keys are not passed on to other apps: with Ctrl+left click as a hotkey, other apps do not get Ctrl+left click while PuppyMacro runs. The hotkey of a loop or macro with a Specific app is taken only while that app is in front; in other apps it works as usual.
  • Two loops or macros can have the same hotkey only when they work in different apps, or one in an app and the other in all apps: the one for the app in front wins. Stop all, Overlay mode and Record work in every app, so no loop or macro can use them.
